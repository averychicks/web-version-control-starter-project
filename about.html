Musical Leitmotifs

A leitmotif, in musical composition, is a recurring melody that can represent a variety of things. Whether it be a person, a place, a thing, a feeling, an idea, etc.

How the leitmotif is used can differ depending on the given context and scenario of the scene it's associated with. Because it's just the composed melody, it can be used in a variety of styles and genres to fit the scene while still being associated with the thing it represents.

Example: UNDERTALE (Video Game)

UNDERTALE, released for PC systems in 2015, is an indie-RPG made by one person, Toby Fox. He also solely composed the music for the game himself. For the game's music, he aimed to present reoccuring leitmotifs that could be used to represent the player's progression as they continue through the game.

For example, the game's opening track "Once Upon a Time", played during the game's opening prologue, sets the tone for the narrative of the game by clearly presenting the melody to make it instantly familiar to the listener.

UNDERTALE Soundtrack - Once Upon a Time

Much later in the game, the game's final boss theme "Hopes and Dreams" reuses the main melody of "Once Upon a Time" in a much different, rock-like style, while also incorporating the motif of a character in the game, all the while providing its own unique melody.
